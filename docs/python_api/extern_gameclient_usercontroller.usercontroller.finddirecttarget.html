extern_gameclient_usercontroller.UserController.FindDirectTarget Method

Definition

Finds the targetable entity under the reticle.

FindDirectTarget()

Returns

The entity id (long), or None.

Remarks

A 100 m ray from the camera through the reticle, shortened by the terrain; the nearest pickable body it hits (not the player's own, within 100 m of the player and farther from the camera than the player) - its id when that body is targetable.

Engine
